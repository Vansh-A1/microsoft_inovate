# ClearLedger acceptance inputs

The local hackathon demo is functioning with fictional company identities,
references and policies, preserved source/evaluation history, computed finance
controls and the accepted local research extraction runtime. This does not
establish a production pilot, real organization authentication, successful malware
scanning, representative extraction accuracy or a real-company allowance policy.
No payment execution is included.

| Input needed | Current local evidence | External input and acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful independent invoice/expense corpus | Frozen original fictional layouts and one privately authorized invoice; limited field/layout measurements | Permission to process anonymized original sources locally, plus independently adjudicated facts/statuses. Target 40–60 varied native/scanned, multi-page, multi-column, wrapped/repeated-item layouts, date/currency ambiguity and absent/illegible fields. Reserve vendor/layout families before tuning; do not reuse inspected cases as unseen. |
| Source truth and review authority | Unknown/ambiguous values cannot become finance clearance; corrections create versions with actor/reason/source | Named adjudicators and authorized sources for missing quantities, tax treatment and accounting semantics. Preserve page/region or source-reference citations, not guessed zeros/FX/tax conventions. Decide treatment when an invoice is actually blank; leave unknown without evidence. |
| Business policies | Fictional hotel INR 8000→9000 version/effective-date/audit demonstration; retained old reports | Approved entity-specific currencies, allowances, effective dates, tax/accounting treatment, expense eligibility, approval hierarchy/delegations, vendor/employee references, budgets and receipt/PO capacity. Verify staged validation/activation and historical replay with business owners; no universal corporate policy is inferred. |
| Organization identity and roles | Local sample identity selector; server-side tenant/entity/role checks and denied access tests | Approved Entra tenant/application configuration, server-owned membership-to-entity/role mappings, worker identities and separation-of-duties owners. Complete real sign-in, expiry, revocation, cross-tenant and authorization acceptance privately. Supply secrets through approved private configuration, not chat/Git/logs. |
| Malware engine and definitions | Adapter path and fail-closed required-scanning behavior exist; local demo explicitly permits preprocessing without a configured engine | Operator-approved scanner executable/service and definition update ownership, engine/definition provenance and real clean/infected/unavailable drills. Configuration is not a clean verdict; unavailable/failed scanning cannot permit required-scanning intake. No engine is silently installed. |
| Extraction model/provider acceptance | Actual pinned local Qwen/TypeLLM/BF16 provenance, CPU OCR and measured limits; human fallback | Resolve commercial model attribution/use and organization data-processing/residency approval. Any stronger provider or external transmission needs explicit authorization and a bounded compatibility/quality/timeout test. The absence of an approved provider means human review, not fixture substitution. |
| Performance and recovery targets | Scoped durable worker/idempotency/concurrency/recovery tests and single-host cold/warm/pipeline/queue measurements | Agreed workload, size/page limits, quality/latency targets and suitable infrastructure. Repeated cold-weight, first-worker and resident measurements, per-process/whole-device memory attribution, concurrency/timeout/recovery drills. Current shared-host runs do not establish a throughput SLA. |
| Deployment/release inputs | Local CPU/cloud artifacts and historical release gates; no ClearLedger publication request | Explicit release approval, subscription/region/permissions/spend ceiling, approved identities/network/residency/storage/recovery owners and reviewed parameters. Provisioning/public deployment/Git publication remain separate actions. No credential, firewall, driver or Docker repair follows from this checklist. |

No restricted or token-required dataset is fetched for acceptance. Private invoices
stay in authorized local storage; they are not committed, exported to Library or
sent to an external inference service. Fictional fixtures remain identified as such.
The current rules/anomaly governance outcome does not justify supervised risk
training: representative adjudicated risk labels remain a separate mandatory gate.

Before accepting a production pilot, record the actual owner-approved inputs,
executed evidence and remaining failures for each applicable row. Do not replace
missing inputs with demo data or a declaration of readiness. For the hackathon,
show the proven local workflow and disclose the applicable missing inputs plainly.
